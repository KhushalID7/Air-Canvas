Gesture Guide

☝️
Index finger Move mouse cursor
🤏
Pinch (thumb + index) Left click
✌️
Two fingers — upper half Scroll up
✌️
Two fingers — lower half Scroll down
🖖
Three fingers Next tab (Ctrl+Tab)
👈
Open palm — swipe left Go back (Alt+←)
👉
Open palm — swipe right Go forward (Alt+→)
✊
Fist Minimize window (Win+↓)
✋
Open palm (still) Idle — no action
⚠️ The backend executes mouse & keyboard actions on your local machine. Actions apply to whichever window was active just before you made the gesture.
